## Question

What question are we trying to answer?

## Hypothesis

What do we expect and why?

## Background

What prior work, assumptions, or observations motivate this?

## Data

Describe datasets, sources, versions, and provenance.

## Method

Describe the experiment or evaluation plan.

## Baseline

What baseline or comparison will be used?

## Metrics

What measurements determine the result?

## Risks and Limitations

Identify likely sources of bias, leakage, confounding, or failure.

## Expected Deliverables

List the expected outputs.

## Next Step

What is the smallest useful first experiment?

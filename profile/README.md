# qu4n-ai

**AI · Software Engineering · Quantitative Research**

qu4n-ai is a personal research and engineering organization exploring and building across modern technology.

## Areas of Exploration

- Artificial intelligence and machine learning
- LLMs, ASR, and model fine-tuning
- Full-stack applications and developer tools
- MLOps, infrastructure, and deployment
- Quantitative finance and algorithmic research
- Backtesting, experimentation, and data analysis

## Engineering Principles

- Build reproducible systems.
- Document important decisions.
- Validate assumptions with tests and experiments.
- Protect private data and credentials.
- Prefer simple, maintainable solutions.
- Improve through continuous research and iteration.

## Development Model

qu4n-ai is **private-first** and currently operated as a solo workflow.

Projects may run locally, on self-hosted infrastructure, or in cloud environments depending on requirements.

## Repository Convention

- `ai-*` — AI/ML systems and models
- `app-*` — applications and products
- `quant-*` — quantitative research and trading systems
- `research-*` — experiments and research
- `infra-*` — infrastructure and deployment
- `lib-*` — reusable libraries and tooling
- `*-docs` — documentation and standards

## Status

Building the foundation of the qu4n-ai research and engineering ecosystem.
